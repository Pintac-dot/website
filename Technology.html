Faith and Technology

Using Technology to Spread Light

Technology gives us many ways to communicate with people and love ones around the world. We can use websites, social media, videos, and other digital tools to share our messages and encourage others.

Sharing Positivity

We can use technology to share encouraging words, inspiration messages, helpful information, and stories that can bring hope to other student and people.

Sharing Faith

Digital or technology can also be use to share good messages and reminders about faith, kindness, forgiveness, and love.

Helping Others Through Technology

We use technology to organize school or community activities, share information about ways to help, and connect people who want to make a good difference.

Una ang Ginoo

Putting God first means using our abilities and technology in a responsible way. Instead of using technology to spread negativity, we can use it to inspire, teach, and help other people and students.

Be a Light

Every message we share online is an opportunity to make a good difference. Lets use technology wisely and let our digital actions become the light of others.

Faith + Technology = Positive Impact,difference and motivation
