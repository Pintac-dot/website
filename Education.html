Digital Ethics

Be a Light Online

The internet is a powerful tool for learning, communication, and creativity. Using this, we should use technology responsibly and treat other people with kindness and respect.

Think before posting or commenting. Avoid hurtful words and respect other people's opinions and feelings.

2. Be Honest

Dont copy some person's work and claim it as your own. Give proper credit when using someones idea, image, or informations.

3. Think Before You Share

Check informations before sharing it online. False informations can mislead conflict,dabate or hurt other people.

4. Protect Your Privacy

Avoid sharing private information such as passwords, personal address, or other important information in online.

Our Digital Responsibility

Technology should be used to learn, help,and inspire others. Let our actions online cause kindness, honesty, and respect.
